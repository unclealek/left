## 2. Our Privacy Principles

Left is designed around several core principles:

### Location should serve a visible product purpose

We process location primarily to operate venue presence, nearby discovery, venue identification and related safety/privacy features.

### Precise location is not shown to ordinary users

Left does not ordinarily disclose your raw GPS coordinates to other users.

### Presence should be temporary

Presence-session information is generally retained for no longer than 24 hours unless there is a legitimate need for limited additional retention.

### Social discovery should not become surveillance

Left is not designed to provide users with a permanent history of another person's movements.

### We do not sell precise location data

We do not sell your precise location.

### We do not use precise/background location for third-party advertising

Location information collected for Left's core functionality is not used by us for third-party behavioural advertising.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account Information

When you authenticate with Google, we may receive:

* first name;
* last name;
* email address;
* Google account/authentication identifier; and
* authentication-related technical information.

We do not currently require users to upload real profile photographs.

If your Google account contains a profile photograph, Left does not intentionally use that photograph as your Left profile photo unless this product behaviour changes and is disclosed to you.

## 3.2 Precise Location

With your permission, Left may access precise device location.

This may include latitude and longitude or equivalent coordinates.

We use precise location to:

* detect nearby venues;
* associate you with a venue;
* determine presence;
* estimate approximate distance;
* improve venue matching;
* determine when a presence session should start or end;
* prevent stale presence information; and
* support safety and privacy functionality.

Precise coordinates are not ordinarily disclosed to other Left users.

## 3.3 Approximate Location

Left may process approximate geographic information such as:

* approximate area;
* neighbourhood;
* nearby venue area;
* reduced-precision coordinates; or
* proximity classifications.

Approximate information may be shown to other users where necessary for social-discovery functionality.

## 3.4 Background Location

If you grant background or “Always” location permission, Left may access location while:

* the application is running in the background;
* the application is not actively visible;
* your device detects significant location changes; or
* other permitted background-location mechanisms are active.

Background location is used to support core functionality such as:

* maintaining accurate temporary venue presence;
* recognizing meaningful venue entry or exit;
* ending stale presence;
* keeping venue association accurate;
* applying safety zones;
* respecting visibility controls; and
* preventing your profile from incorrectly appearing at a venue after you leave.

We do not use background location for third-party advertising.

You can revoke background location permission at any time through your device settings.

Some functionality may become unavailable or less accurate after revocation.

## 3.5 Significant Location Changes

Where supported by your operating system and permission settings, Left may use significant-location-change functionality to determine when a meaningful movement may require your venue/presence status to be updated.

This does not mean that Left is intended to create a permanent chronological diary of everywhere you travel.

## 3.6 Bluetooth and Beacon Information

If supported by the product and permission settings, Left may access nearby Bluetooth or beacon signals to improve venue or proximity detection.

We use such signals only where relevant to providing location/proximity functionality, security or reliability.

Left does not disclose raw Bluetooth/beacon identifiers to ordinary users.

## 3.7 Presence Information

When you appear to be at or near a venue, Left may create a presence session.

A presence session may include:

* internal user ID;
* venue identifier;
* temporary coordinates;
* approximate area;
* presence start time;
* presence end time;
* visibility setting;
* status;
* technical confidence information; and
* other data required to maintain the session.

Presence sessions are generally retained for **up to 24 hours**.

## 3.8 Short-Lived Historical Location Records

Technical operation of the Service may result in recent location or presence records existing temporarily.

These records may represent recent movement or venue associations.

Left does **not** intend to offer a permanent user-facing location-history product.

We distinguish between:

* temporary operational location records required to maintain presence; and
* a long-term profile of a user's movements.

The former may be processed. The latter is not an intended Left feature.

Operational location records will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for their stated purpose, generally within the 24-hour presence window unless another lawful reason requires limited retention.

## 3.9 Visibility and Privacy Settings

We collect settings such as:

* visible/hidden status;
* location preferences;
* safety-zone configuration;
* permission state where available;
* notification preferences; and
* related privacy choices.

## 3.10 Safety Zones

Where you use safety zones, Left may process information necessary to determine whether you are within a location where your social-discovery visibility should be reduced or disabled.

We design safety zones to minimize unnecessary disclosure of sensitive location information.

Where technically feasible, safety-zone data should be stored or processed in a manner designed to reduce unnecessary exposure of the underlying location.

## 3.11 Saved Venues and Product Preferences

Where enabled, we may collect:

* saved venues;
* preferred places;
* venue interactions;
* feature preferences; and
* other settings you intentionally select.

## 3.12 Venue Information

Left obtains venue information from third-party services.

This may include:

* venue name;
* address;
* category;
* coordinates;
* Google Place ID;
* imagery;
* activity forecasts;
* operating information; and
* related place information.

Venue information is generally not information collected *about you*, although your interaction with or presence at a venue can become personal data associated with your account.

## 3.13 Google Places Information

Left uses Google Places API (New), including Place Details and Place Photos.

We may request venue information from Google to show appropriate places and images.

Google may process technical information in connection with those requests according to Google's terms and privacy policies.

Left does not intentionally permanently store Google Place Photos where storage is prohibited.

Google Place IDs may be retained where allowed.

## 3.14 BestTime

Left uses BestTime to obtain or generate venue-level information such as:

* typical popularity;
* forecast activity;
* peak periods;
* live or predicted venue density where available.

BestTime information generally concerns venues rather than identified Left users.

We may combine this venue-level information with Left's own product logic to generate social-density indicators.

## 3.15 Technical Information

To operate and secure the Service, we may process technical information automatically generated by normal application and server operation, including where applicable:

* IP address;
* timestamps;
* application version;
* operating system;
* device type;
* request logs;
* authentication logs;
* server logs;
* error information;
* security events; and
* diagnostic information.

We will update this Policy and applicable App Store/Google Play disclosures before introducing materially different analytics, advertising or tracking practices.

## 11. Retention

We do not keep personal information indefinitely merely because storage is technically possible.

## 12. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Left account.

Where available, use:

**Settings → Account → Delete Account**

Deleting the Left application from your phone does not itself delete your Left account.

When we receive a valid deletion request, we will delete or irreversibly anonymise account-linked personal data that we no longer need.

This ordinarily includes:

* first name;
* last name;
* email address;
* Left profile;
* authentication association;
* Google Sign-In association maintained by Left;
* privacy preferences;
* visibility settings;
* saved venues;
* active presence;
* presence sessions still linked to you;
* temporary location records;
* account-linked approximate location information;
* safety-zone configuration where retained;
* notification tokens;
* user preferences; and
* other ordinary account-linked product information.

Deletion will not necessarily remove information that:

* has already been lawfully anonymised;
* must be retained by law;
* is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
* is required for security or fraud prevention;
* is necessary to maintain records of serious abuse or bans;
* is subject to a lawful preservation request; or
* temporarily remains in protected backups.

Where information is retained after deletion for these reasons, we will restrict its use to the relevant purpose.

## 13. Revoking Google Authentication

## 14. Location Permission Withdrawal

You can withdraw location access from your device settings.

Depending on the permission you revoke:

* precise location may become approximate;

* venue presence may become less accurate;

Withdrawing permission stops future access permitted by that operating-system permission. It does not automatically require deletion of information lawfully collected before withdrawal.

You may separately request deletion where applicable.
